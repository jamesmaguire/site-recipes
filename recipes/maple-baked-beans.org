#+TITLE: Maple baked beans
#+DATE: 2026-08-01
#+SETUPFILE: recipe.setup

[[https://www.booktopia.com.au/the-kindness-community-vegan-cookbook-edgar-s-mission/book/9781922400307.html][Original link]]

* Ingredients
  - 1 can borlotti beans (400g)
  - 1 can butter beans (400g)
  - 1 can diced tomatoes (400g)
  - 1 clove garlic, minced
  - 1 1/2 tbsp smoked paprika
  - 1/4 tsp ground chipotle
  - 2 tbsp maple syrup
  - 1 tsp vegan worcestershire sauce
  - Salt and pepper to taste

* Instructions
  1. Drain and rinse the beans.
  2. Put all ingredients into a saucepan and cook on low-medium for 10 minutes or longer.
  3. Serve on toast over a generous layer of avocado.
