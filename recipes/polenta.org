#+TITLE: Polenta
#+DATE: 2026-07-11
#+SETUPFILE: recipe.setup

* Ingredients
- 4 cups boiling water
- 2 tsp stock powder (optional)
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 cup polenta
- 2 tbsp margarine

* Instructions
1. Bring water /broth to a boil
2. Optionally add some water to polenta to mix into a paste
3. Add polenta to boiling water and stir with a whisk to remove clumps
4. Cook for 30 minutes on low heat stirring regularly
5. Mix through margarine, let cool, and eat
